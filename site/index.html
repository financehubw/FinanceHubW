Why The "Safe" Investment Makes You Poorer

FD return after tax and inflation Often negative after tax and inflation

Fixed deposits feel safe because the number never falls. But if inflation is 6% and your deposit pays 6.5% before tax, what is left after tax can be less than inflation, so your money buys a little less each year.

Takeaway

When interest after tax is below inflation, the balance grows while its buying power shrinks.

The 10-year head start

Age 20 vs 30 Multiplier See the calculator above

Many people wait until they earn more before they start investing. But money invested at 22 gets several more doublings before 65 than money invested at 35, and those extra doublings are where most of the growth comes from.

Takeaway

Starting ten years late is very hard to make up with bigger contributions later.

Myth vs fact

Things people repeat about money

Common sayings, checked against the numbers.

Myth Gold

"Gold is always the safest store of wealth."

check_circle Fact

Gold can go nowhere for decades. In US dollars it peaked in 1980 and did not pass that level again until 2008, and it pays no income along the way.

Gold can protect in a crisis, but it does not grow with the economy.
Myth Small savings

"Saving ₹3,000 a month is too little to matter."

check_circle Fact

At 10% a year for 35 years, ₹3,000 a month grows to roughly ₹1.1 crore from ₹12.6 lakh put in. Most of the final amount is growth, not deposits.

Time in the market does more than the size of late contributions.
Quick quiz

What does $1,000 of 1970 spending cost today?

The same basket of food, transport, energy and housing, bought at today's US prices.

check_circle Right!

According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Consumer Price Index (CPI-U), US prices rose about 710% from 1970 to 2024. A basket priced at $1,000 requires over $8,120 today. If stored in physical cash under a mattress, that $1,000 lost 87.7% of its real capacity to feed, house, and transport your family.

Source: St. Louis FRED (CPIAUCSL)
